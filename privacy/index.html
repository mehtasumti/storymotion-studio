The short version

  • There are no accounts and no StoryMotion server that receives your stories, images or audio.
  • Your project is saved only in your browser, on your device. Videos and PDFs are rendered on your device.
  • Optional AI images are off by default. If you turn them on and click Generate, that scene’s image prompt is sent to Pollinations, a third-party service, using your own key.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies. It doesn’t see what you type or upload.
  • No analytics are currently enabled on this site.
  • Feedback goes through a Google Form, only if you choose to open and submit it.

1. What’s stored in your browser

StoryMotion Studio is a set of static files that runs entirely in your web browser. To keep your work between visits, it uses your browser’s own storage on your device. StoryMotion can’t see this storage, and nothing in it is sent to us.

Browser storage used by StoryMotion Studio
WhereNameWhat it holds
Local storage storymotion-studio:project:v1 Your story, title, character description, settings, the storyboard with your edits, and video settings (camera moves, captions, volumes). It holds references to media, not the media itself.
Local storage storymotion-studio:project:v1:corrupt-backup Only created if a saved project can’t be read. It keeps a raw copy so your work isn’t silently lost.
Local storage storymotion-studio:ai-prefs Whether you turned on AI images, that you accepted the notice, and your chosen model name.
Local storage storymotion-studio:ai-key Your Pollinations key, only if you tick “Remember”. Otherwise the key is kept in memory and forgotten when you close the tab.
IndexedDB storymotion-studio-media The images and audio files you add (and AI images you generate), stored as files on your device.

Exported files (JSON projects, PDF storyboards and videos) are created on your device and saved wherever your browser saves downloads. JSON exports contain your story and storyboard but not your images or audio.

2. Images and audio you add

When you upload images, music or voice recordings, the files are read, resized (very large images are scaled down to 2560 pixels on the long edge) and stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. They are decoded, previewed, mixed and encoded into your video on your own device. StoryMotion never uploads them anywhere, and they’re never sent to Pollinations.

The demo project loads six sample illustrations from this website and stores copies in the same local storage, so you can edit and export them like your own images.

3. Optional AI images (Pollinations)

The Video maker can ask Pollinations, an independent third-party image service, to draw a scene. This is off by default. It only works after you tick a consent box, turn it on and paste a key from your own free Pollinations account. Nothing is sent until you click Generate, Regenerate or Generate missing.

What is sent, per image

  • The scene’s image prompt, up to 1,500 characters. It usually includes a sentence or phrase from that scene of your story, your character description, and style, mood and lighting words. You can read and edit it in the storyboard before generating.
  • The image width and height, a number (“seed”) that keeps results repeatable, the model name if you chose one, and a “no logo” setting.
  • Your Pollinations key, in an authorization header. If your browser blocks that request, the app retries once with the key in the web address, which Pollinations supports for image requests.

Like any website you connect to, Pollinations also receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. The app sends requests without cookies and without a referrer. Your full story, other scenes’ text, uploaded images and audio are not sent.

The images Pollinations returns are stored only in your browser. Pollinations’ handling of prompts, images and account data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, not this notice, so please review them on pollinations.ai before using the feature. Turn off AI images in the app deletes your saved key and AI settings from this browser. It can’t delete anything already sent to Pollinations.

4. Hosting

The site is served as static files by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers). There’s no StoryMotion server code that stores visitor information. As with any website, delivering pages means Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser user agent and the page requested, to serve the site and protect it from abuse. The site owner can see aggregated traffic figures in the Cloudflare dashboard. Cloudflare’s handling of this data is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Fonts and icons are bundled with the site, so no requests go to font or icon services.

5. Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall usage, such as how many people visit, which pages they view, where visits come from (referrer), broad device, browser and country information, and page-load performance. A small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com sends this to Cloudflare on each page view.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics doesn’t use cookies or local storage to collect metrics, doesn’t fingerprint visitors, and doesn’t log URL query strings. It never sees your story, storyboard, images, audio or AI key, because those never leave your browser through it. Ad or tracker blockers may block the script. That’s fine, and the app works the same either way.

Analytics are not enabled on this site right now, and no analytics script is loaded. If that changes, we plan to use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies, and we’ll update this section before turning it on.

6. Feedback

The Send feedback buttons open a Google Form in a new tab. Nothing is sent unless you fill it in and submit it. Whatever you submit, and any information Google collects (for example if you’re signed in to a Google account, or if the form asks for an e-mail address), is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and shared with the site owner as form responses.

Please don’t include sensitive personal information, passwords or API keys in feedback. Feedback is used only to fix problems and improve StoryMotion Studio.

A feedback form isn’t connected to this build yet. When it is, it will be a Google Form that opens only when you click Send feedback, and this section will explain how responses are handled.

7. What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account, e-mail address or payment.
  • The app’s code doesn’t set cookies.
  • We don’t run advertising or ad trackers, and we don’t sell or share personal data for advertising.
  • We don’t upload, read or train AI models on your stories, images or audio.

8. Deleting your data

Your data is on your device, so you’re in control:

  • Reset in the storyboard toolbar clears your story, storyboard, images, audio and AI settings from this browser.
  • Turn off AI images removes your saved Pollinations key and AI preferences.
  • Clearing site data for this website in your browser settings removes everything StoryMotion stored.

Deleted local data can’t be recovered by us, because we never had a copy. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep your work.

9. Security tips

  • Anyone with access to your browser profile can open your saved project. Use Reset on shared or public computers.
  • If you tick “Remember” for your Pollinations key, it’s stored unencrypted in this browser’s local storage. Leave it unticked on shared devices, and revoke the key in your Pollinations account if you think it was exposed.
  • Private or incognito windows may block storage, in which case your work disappears when you close the window.
