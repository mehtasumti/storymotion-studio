What it does

Animates your still images with zoom, pan, tilt and drift moves, plus transitions, captions, music and voice.

What it doesn’t

It does not generate AI video. Characters and objects do not move on their own, and there’s no lip-sync and no generated voices.

Storyboard creator

Paste your story and choose a style, genre, length and aspect ratio. You get 4 to 10 scenes, each with a shot type, camera direction, image prompt, image-to-video prompt, dialogue, voiceover, music and sound notes, and a transition. Durations always add up to your target length.

Storyboards are written by rules and templates, not an AI model, so the same story always gives the same result. Everything is editable.

Video maker

Add one image per scene: upload your own, or optionally generate one with your own free Pollinations key. StoryMotion animates each still with a camera move, follows your transitions, burns in captions, mixes music, narration and dialogue, and exports WebM or MP4.

The preview and the exported file use the same renderer, so what you see is what you download.

Features

Everything a short story video needs

  • Timed, editable storyboard

    4, 6, 8 or 10 scenes over 30, 60 or 90 seconds. Edit any field. Lock the total and the other scenes rebalance automatically.

  • Ready-to-use prompts

    Image prompts and image-to-video prompts for every scene, with a shared character description to keep people consistent.

  • Camera moves on stills

    Zoom in or out, pan, tilt, cinematic drift or static. “Auto” reads each scene’s camera direction. Pans never show empty edges.

  • Captions that fit

    Built from dialogue or voiceover. Choose size, position, style and colour. Long lines are split into readable two-line pages.

  • Music, narration & dialogue

    Background music with fades and looping, voice clips per scene, separate levels, automatic ducking and a limiter to prevent clipping.

  • Your images, or optional AI

    Drag and drop, swap or batch-add images. Optional AI images use your own free Pollinations key. It’s off by default and only image prompts are sent.

  • Verified exports

    WebM or MP4 at 720p or 1080p, plus PDF storyboards and JSON backups. Each video is checked before you download it.

  • Private by design

    No account and no backend. Your story, images and audio are saved in your own browser and never uploaded by StoryMotion.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Is StoryMotion Studio really free?

Yes. There’s no account, subscription or paid API behind it. The only optional outside service is Pollinations for AI images, which uses a key from your own free Pollinations account. Their free limits and terms are set by them and can change.

Does it create AI-generated video?

No. Each scene is one still image with a camera move (zoom, pan, tilt or drift), plus transitions, captions and your audio. Characters and objects don’t move on their own. If you want true AI motion, the storyboard includes image-to-video prompts you can paste into separate tools, which have their own pricing.

Is the storyboard written by AI?

No. A rule-and-template engine splits your story into beats and writes shot directions and prompts. It never invents dialogue. Short stories are padded with clearly labelled “derived” beats, and you can edit everything.

Where is my work saved?

In your own browser: the project in local storage, and images and audio in IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to a StoryMotion server, because there isn’t one. Clearing your browser data erases it, so use Export JSON for backups and keep your original media files.

Can I try it without writing a story?

Yes. Open the demo project to load a six-scene mystery with illustrated images already in place. You can play it, change camera moves and captions, and export a video straight away.

Which browsers can export video?

Recent desktop Chrome and Edge export WebM and usually MP4, faster than real time. Firefox exports WebM, and MP4 if your system has the right encoders. Safari support depends on the version. The app tests your browser and only offers formats that work. Phones work too, but 720p is recommended.

Who owns the videos I make?

You keep your rights to your story and media. You’re responsible for having the rights to any images and music you add, and for following the terms of any outside tools you use. See the terms of use.

What does “beta” mean here?

The app is new and still changing. Expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything important. Your feedback shapes what comes next.
